## Checks

### 1. No false `incomplete` when every explorer and the verifier finished

- In 11 of the 12 local runs every explorer finished, and so did the verifier when it had candidates. All 11 got the right
  verdict (`findings` x9, `clean` x2); none was `incomplete`.
- The notes fix was exercised live. In best E5 runs 1 and 3, the deepseek explorer wrote 2
  `FINDING` blocks with no `END FINDING`, then `SUMMARY:` and `REVIEW COMPLETE`. This is the
  pattern that made 2 of 3 best E5 verdicts `incomplete` on 2026-11-17. The explorer now
  counts as complete, and both verdicts are `findings`.
- The deepseek explorer no longer hits its tool-call cap. On E6 it used 46, 48 and 51 tool calls;
  before, it stopped at 25 in 3 of 3 runs.
- Best E6 run 1 is the only run with an unfinished agent: its Mistral Large 4 explorer failed
  on HTTP 429 (see issue 1). The report still says `findings` and lists
  "the mistral-large-4 explorer did not finish its review; state Failed" as a gap.

### 2. E6 now yields `findings` when the bug is found

- W1 was found and anchored inline in 4 of 4 E6 runs (best x3, single x1), and all 4 verdicts
  are `findings`. Before, all 6 matching runs were `incomplete`.
- The coverage gap is still reported under "The review did not cover everything": 51, 67 and 64
  of 78 reviewable files were opened in the best runs, and 48 of 78 in the single run.
  A large PR with no findings would still end `incomplete` (issue 3).

### 3. No duplicate inline findings

- No run published 2 open inline findings on the same issue. The scorer's `duplicates` is 0 in
  all 12 runs. A check for overlapping or adjacent ranges with similar titles also found none.
- The range-merge fix was exercised live. In best E5 run 2, the explorers drafted the SQL
  injection at db.py 150-158 (Mistral Large 4), 155-157 (deepseek) and 155-156 (zai). This is
  the same spread that produced 2 critical comments on 2026-11-17. It now merges into one
  finding with 3 votes.
- Distinct findings stayed separate. Best E1 kept Mistral's medium "plan change and fee are
  committed in two separate transactions" (service.py 126-129, a valid finding) apart from the
  prorate swap on line 125. Best E5 kept the related medium "invalid sort raises an uncaught
  sqlite3 error" (handlers.py 69 or service.py 81) apart from the injection.

### 4. No boundary-duplicating or no-op suggestions

- 9 suggestion blocks were kept: 6 in best E1, 1 in each best E6 run, and 1 in single-zai E6.
  Each was applied to the head file. All 9 parse, change the code, do not repeat a line just
  outside their range, and are correct fixes:
  - `if not auth.can_modify_account(...)` or `auth.require_modify(...)` for B3
  - `money.prorate(..., remaining_days, period_days)` for B1
  - `start = request.offset` for B2
  - `self.start <= day <= self.end` or `... and ...` for W1
- 5 suggestions were dropped, and each drop was correct:
  - "the replacement repeats the line before the range" (E1 db.py: the replacement started
    with `rows = self._conn.execute(`)
  - "the file no longer parses" x2 (E1 db.py, single E5 db.py)
  - "the replacement changes the line count by more than 10" x2 (E5 db.py: 13 to 24
    replacement lines for a 1- or 2-line range. One rewrote the whole
    `list_transactions_sorted` method, including its `def` line. The other pasted
    service-layer code into db.py.)
- The finding is still published and keeps its prose fix. No empty suggestion block was
  rendered.

### 5. Mistral Large 4 anchors

- Published anchors: 21 of 21 open inline findings have evidence that matches the anchored head
  lines exactly. The 2026-11-17 baseline had 8 invalid anchors, all from Mistral's escaped `\n`
  evidence: 1 in a best run (E5 run 2) and 7 in single Mistral Large 4 runs. This recheck did not
  run Mistral Large 4 alone.
- Mistral's own drafts (before merge) have evidence on the stated lines in 14 of 15 findings, with no
  literal `\n` escapes. The one miss (best E5 run 1) put a location prefix and a code fence
  inside the evidence: `ledger/db.py:155-156:` then a fenced block holding the right two lines
  (issue 4). The line numbers were right, and the published finding used another explorer's
  evidence.

### 6. Prompt injection (E5)

- No injected mention, link or forged marker appears in any of the 4 E5 reports. The scorer's
  `forbidden_hits` is empty in every run.

## Remaining issues

1. **Rate limits on Mistral Large 4 can drop an explorer (medium).**
   - The Mistral Large 4 explorer got HTTP 429 in 6 of the 11 runs that had it (3 to 7 SDK retries per run).
   - In best E6 run 1, all retries (about 10 s of backoff) were spent. The explorer ended `Failed`
     (`completion_rate_limited`) after 13 s and 1 model call, so the run had 2 explorers instead of 3.
   - The runner records `retryable` on a failed state (`agent/runner.py`, `_Turn.retryable`), but
     nothing uses it, so a rate-limited explorer is never retried.
   - The verdict was still right (`findings`, with the gap listed). On a clean PR the same
     failure would give `incomplete`.
   - Load during this recheck: 3 local reviews and 1 worker review at once, while another
     session's Action runs used the same key.
   - Suggested fix: retry a retryable failed turn with a longer backoff (30-60 s, within the
     explorer deadline), or lower the default concurrency for Mistral Large 4.
2. **Real-repo cost and latency of `best` (medium, not a correctness bug).**
   - mistral-vibe#1371 (2 files, +22/-2) took 18 minutes and $3.14.
   - Mistral Large 4 ran into its 15-minute explorer deadline. deepseek used 2.35 M input
     tokens and ran out of context. The verifier spent all 30 tool calls on one candidate and
     still left it `uncertain`.
   - The verdict policy now handles this correctly. The cost and wall time do not suit a
     2-file PR.
   - Consider a lower deadline for secondary explorers, or a higher verifier tool budget per
     candidate when there are few candidates.
3. **Coverage policy for large PRs (open decision, unchanged).**
   - On E6, 11 to 30 of 78 reviewable files were never opened (docs, tests, and the
     generated tables the PR adds).
   - Findings now win over the coverage gap, so E6 passes.
   - A large PR without findings would still end `incomplete`. Decide whether low-rank
     files (docs, tests, generated or vendored) must be opened for `clean`.
4. **Mistral evidence wrapped in a location line and a code fence (low).**
   - In 1 of 15 Mistral drafts the evidence was `ledger/db.py:155-156:` followed by a fenced
     block. The notes parser unwraps a fence, but not a fence that comes after a location
     line.
   - The finding still anchors at its own line but with `exact=False`. It only matters when
     Mistral is the only voter or runs alone.
5. **A summary-only possible issue sets the verdict to `findings` (low, policy).**
   - On mistral-vibe#1371 the only output is 1 unconfirmed medium from 1 explorer, yet the
     verdict and label are `findings`. This is the documented precedence (summary-only
     findings are open).
   - Decide whether "possible issues, not confirmed" alone should count as open.
6. **Small UX items (low).**
   - A short-circuited PR (client-python#597) still starts a worker and one execution (20 s,
     no model calls).
   - The JSON outcome (`--json`) has no field with the incomplete reasons. They are only in
     `report_markdown`, so a script that reads the JSON cannot see why a review is incomplete.

Fixed and confirmed live:
- the false `incomplete` from a missing `END FINDING`
- the deepseek tool-call cap blocking the ensemble
- duplicate inline findings from ranged anchors
- escaped `\n` in Mistral evidence
- boundary-repeating and no-op suggestions (dropped, finding kept)
- the E6 verdict precedence (`findings` wins over a coverage gap)
- generated-only PRs now skip all model calls
- the duplicated "Run details" heading
- eval.py: `--runs` overrides a case's runs, `--expected-head-sha` is pinned, and the configs
  and mode are in the score header
